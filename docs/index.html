Your desk is the keyboard.

Point your webcam at the table, tap where the keys would be, and the letters appear below. Everything runs in this tab. No video leaves your computer.

Aim the camera at your desk

A laptop webcam normally looks at your face. Tilt the screen forward, or use a USB webcam pointing down, so both hands fit in the picture, wrists included.

Camera off

Turn on the camera to begin.

What you typed

Raise it if taps are missed. Lower it if keys appear while you rest.

How to type

  1. Place the keyboard. Drag the four round corners until the keys sit under your fingertips. If the space bar is on the far side, press Flip rows.
  2. Start typing. You get a moment to put your hands down, then the light turns green.
  3. Rest, then tap. Hold your fingers still for a second, touch the table and lift. Each fingertip shows the key it is over, and its small bar fills as you press.

Thumbs only type Space. Shift and Caps work as toggles: tap Shift, then the letter.

Type into any app

This page types into itself. The desktop app runs in the background and types into whatever window you are using: press Ctrl Alt K, tap, press it again to stop. It works on Linux, Windows and macOS, and can use an Android phone as an overhead camera.
