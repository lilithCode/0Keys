★ Webcam keyboard

Your desk is the keyboard.

Point your webcam at the table and tap where the keys would be. Runs in this tab — no video leaves your computer.

Turn on camera How it works →

/ Setup

Aim the camera at your desk

Tilt your laptop screen forward or use a USB webcam pointing down — both hands in frame, wrists included.

Camera off

Turn on the camera to begin.

/ Output

Raise if taps are missed. Lower if keys fire while resting.

/ How it works

Three steps

  1. 01

    Place the keyboard

    Drag the four corners until the keys sit under your fingertips. Space bar on the far side? Press Flip rows.

  2. 02

    Start typing

    Hit Start typing and put your hands down. The light turns green when it’s ready.

  3. 03

    Rest, then tap

    Hold still for a second, then touch the table and lift. Each fingertip shows the key it’s over.

Tip Thumbs type Space. Shift and Caps are toggles — tap Shift, then the letter.

/ Desktop app

Type into any app

Runs in the background on Linux, Windows and macOS. Press Ctrl Alt K to start, again to stop. It can even use an Android phone as an overhead camera.
