Type on your desk with with your webcam

Point your webcame at your hands on the table and type as if you were using a normal keybard

Turn on camera How it works

Set up your camera

Tilt your laptop screen forward or point a USB webcam down at the desk. Keep both hands and wrists in view.

Camera off

Turn on the camera to begin.

Typed text

Raise it if taps are missed. Lower it if resting hands press keys.

How it works

  1. 1

    Place the keyboard

    Drag the four corners until the keys sit where you want them. To put the space bar on the far side, press Flip rows.

  2. 2

    Start typing

    Press Start typing and put your hands in position. The light turns green when it's ready.

  3. 3

    Rest, then tap

    Hold still for a second, then tap the table and lift. Each fingertip shows the key it's over.

Tip: lift your fingers a little between taps so they're detected better.

Desktop

Type into any app on Linux, Windows and macOS. Press Ctrl Alt K to start and again to stop. You can also use an Android phone as the camera for a sharper picture.
